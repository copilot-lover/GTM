# GLOSSARY

- **FACT** observed business data (posting URL, site CTA)
- **INFERENCE** interpreted meaning (hiring → pressure)
- **OPPORTUNITY HYPOTHESIS** reasoned profile WHO + WHAT HAPPENING + PROBLEM + WHY ORBIT + WHY NOW + WHO TO CONTACT + EVIDENCE + WHAT NOT TO ASSUME
- **SIGNAL** detectable business change (hiring, ads, expansion) distinguishable from opportunity
- **INTENT** purpose behind business change (hiring dispatcher = operational intent)
- **GTM Leads** FCU: FIND→UNDERSTAND→QUALIFY→IDENTIFY→OPPORTUNITY — who to pursue
- **GTM Intent** FCU: OBSERVE→SIGNAL→CONTEXT→INTERPRETATION→OPPORTUNITY — what is happening
- **Gate** deterministic 13-check judgment before send
- **P1/P2/P3/P4** priority tiers 85-100 /65-84 /40-64 /<40
- **Fit 0-10** ICP arithmetic total/1.8 threshold 6
- **Suppression** global/ email / phone / company DNC
- **Kill switch** any inbound → pause all automation for lead (FR-12)
- **Research report** citations covering claims via strong-tier LLM + repair loop
- **EMV** expected meeting value `p_reply * p_meeting * value`
- **ORBIT** agency selling AI receptionist / missed-call recovery / booking to local home-services contractors
